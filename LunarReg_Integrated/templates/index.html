Chandrayaan 2 facts

A quick look at India's second lunar exploration mission

Launched July 2019

Chandrayaan-2 lifted off aboard a GSLV Mk III rocket from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre.

Three-part spacecraft

It combined an orbiter, the Vikram lander, and the Pragyan rover in a single mission.

Targeting the south pole

The mission aimed to study a lunar region never explored before, near the Moon's south pole.

Orbiter still active

While the lander's descent did not go as planned, the orbiter continues to map the Moon in detail.
